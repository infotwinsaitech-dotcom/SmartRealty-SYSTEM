ROI Calculator FAQs

What is ROI in real estate? expand_more
ROI (Return on Investment) in real estate measures the profitability of your property investment. It considers both capital appreciation (increase in property value) and rental income, minus all expenses like registration, stamp duty, maintenance, and taxes.
What is a good ROI for property in India? expand_more
A good ROI for residential property in India typically ranges from 10-15% annually, combining both rental yield (3-5%) and capital appreciation (7-10%). Commercial properties may offer higher yields of 6-10%. Location, property type, and market conditions significantly impact ROI.
How is property appreciation calculated? expand_more
Property appreciation is calculated using compound annual growth rate (CAGR). If a property worth ₹50 lakhs appreciates at 8% annually, after 10 years its value will be ₹50L × (1.08)^10 = ₹1.08 crores. Our calculator uses this standard formula for projections.
What expenses should I include in ROI calculation? expand_more
Include all upfront costs (registration 1%, stamp duty 5-6%, legal fees, brokerage) and ongoing expenses (maintenance, property tax, insurance, vacancy periods, repairs). Our calculator defaults to 6% for additional costs, but adjust based on your state and property type.
