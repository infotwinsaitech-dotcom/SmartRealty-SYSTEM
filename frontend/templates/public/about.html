history Our Story

Building Trust Since 2015

RealShree was founded with a simple mission: to make property buying transparent, secure, and hassle-free for every Indian family. What started as a small venture in Ahmedabad has now grown into one of India's most trusted real estate platforms.

We understand that buying a home is not just a financial decision — it's an emotional one. That's why we verify every property listing, ensure RERA compliance, and provide end-to-end support from search to possession.

Our platform serves thousands of buyers, hundreds of builders, and a network of expert agents across 50+ cities. With advanced tools like EMI calculators, ROI analyzers, and property maps, we empower you to make informed decisions.
